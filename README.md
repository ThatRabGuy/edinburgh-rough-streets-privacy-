# Edinburgh Rough Streets Privacy Policy

This repository hosts the privacy policy for the Edinburgh Rough Streets mobile game.

## Setup

1. Edit `index.md` and replace:
   - `[INSERT YOUR EMAIL ADDRESS]` with your contact email
   - `[YOUR NAME/COMPANY NAME]` with your developer name

2. Go to repository **Settings** > **Pages**

3. Under "Build and deployment":
   - Source: **Deploy from a branch**
   - Branch: **main** / **(root)**
   - Click **Save**

4. Wait 2-3 minutes for GitHub to build and deploy

5. Your privacy policy will be available at:
   `https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/edinburgh-rough-streets-privacy/`

6. Use this URL in Google Play Console under **Privacy Policy**

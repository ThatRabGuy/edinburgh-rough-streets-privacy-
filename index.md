# Privacy Policy for Edinburgh Rough Streets

**Last Updated: October 22, 2026**

## Introduction

Edinburgh Rough Streets ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you use our mobile game Edinburgh Rough Streets (the "Game").

## Information We Collect

### Information Collected Automatically

**Google Play Games Services Data**
- When you sign in with Google Play Games, we receive your Google Play Games player ID and display name
- This is used solely for leaderboard functionality
- We do not access your Google account email, contacts, or other personal data

**Purchase Information**
- When you make in-app purchases, Google Play handles all payment processing
- We receive confirmation of purchases but do not collect or store payment details, credit card numbers, or billing addresses
- Purchase records are stored locally on your device

**Advertising Data**
- The Game shows advertisements provided by Google AdMob on its menu screens and after a game ends
- To serve and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate (coarse) location derived from the IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data
- In the UK, EEA and Switzerland you are asked for consent before any advertising data is collected, and you can change your choice at any time from your device's privacy settings
- If you purchase "Remove Ads", no advertisements are shown and no advertising data is collected by the Game
- You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads)

**Crash Reporting & Analytics (Firebase)**
- The Game uses Firebase Crashlytics to automatically collect crash logs, stack traces, and basic device/diagnostic information (e.g. device model, OS version, app version) when the Game crashes or encounters an error, so we can find and fix bugs
- The Game uses Firebase Analytics to collect basic app interaction events (e.g. a chapter or mission was completed, a purchase was made, cloud save synced) to understand how the Game is used and to improve it
- This data is processed by Google's Firebase infrastructure and is not shared with any other third party beyond Google's own subprocessors that help provide the Firebase service
- This data collection is always on and currently cannot be disabled from within the Game

### Information Stored Locally

**Game Progress Data**
- All game progress (money, inventory, statistics, achievements) is stored locally on your device using an encrypted database
- Your progress also syncs to a cloud save slot via Google Play Games Services (Saved Games) when you are signed in, so you can continue your game on a new device or after reinstalling
- Other than cloud save and leaderboard scores, this data never leaves your device

## Information We Do NOT Collect

- Personal identification information (name, email, address, phone number)
- Precise location data
- Photos, contacts, or other device data

## Third-Party Services

### Google Play Games Services
We use Google Play Games Services for leaderboards and cloud save. When you opt in to leaderboards, your score and Google Play Games display name may be visible to other players. Your cloud save (game progress) is stored in your own Google Play Games account and is only accessible to you. Google's Privacy Policy applies to their services: https://policies.google.com/privacy

### Google Play Billing
In-app purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. We do not have access to your payment information. Google's Privacy Policy applies: https://policies.google.com/privacy

### Google AdMob
Advertisements are served by Google AdMob. Google's use of advertising data is described in Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and in "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services" (https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). You can opt out of personalised advertising at https://adssettings.google.com.

### Firebase (Crashlytics & Analytics)
We use Firebase, a Google service, for crash reporting and app analytics as described above. Firebase's privacy and data handling practices are described at https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy and in Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Data Retention

- Game progress is stored locally on your device until you uninstall the Game or clear app data
- Your cloud save (if you're signed in to Google Play Games) is retained in your Google Play Games account according to Google's policies
- Leaderboard scores are retained by Google Play Games according to their policies
- Crash logs and analytics events are retained in Firebase according to Google's data retention policies
- We do not maintain servers that store your personal data

## Children's Privacy

Edinburgh Rough Streets is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The Game contains themes (simulated drug dealing) that are intended for mature audiences.

## Data Security

Your game data is stored locally on your device using Android's Room database. The data transmitted over the internet is leaderboard scores and cloud save data (Google Play Games), purchase verification (Google Play Billing), advertising requests (Google AdMob), and crash/analytics data (Firebase), all over encrypted connections.

## Your Rights

You can:
- Delete all local game data by uninstalling the Game or clearing app data
- Opt out of Google Play Games leaderboards and cloud save at any time by signing out of Google Play Games
- Manage your Google Play Games data through your Google account settings
- Withdraw or change your advertising consent, and remove ads entirely with the "Remove Ads" purchase
- Contact us to ask what data (if any) we hold about you and to request its deletion

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.

# Privacy Policy for Edinburgh Rough Streets

**Last Updated: May 8, 2026**

## Introduction

Edinburgh Rough Streets ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you use our mobile game Edinburgh Rough Streets (the "Game").

## Information We Collect

### Information Collected Automatically

**Google Play Games Services Data**
- When you sign in with Google Play Games, we receive your Google Play Games player ID and display name
- This is used solely for leaderboard functionality
- We do not access your Google account email, contacts, or other personal data

**Purchase Information**
- When you make in-app purchases, Google Play handles all payment processing
- We receive confirmation of purchases but do not collect or store payment details, credit card numbers, or billing addresses
- Purchase records are stored locally on your device

### Information Stored Locally

**Game Progress Data**
- All game progress (money, inventory, statistics, achievements) is stored locally on your device using an encrypted database
- This data never leaves your device except when submitted to Google Play Games leaderboards (score only)

## Information We Do NOT Collect

- Personal identification information (name, email, address, phone number)
- Location data
- Device identifiers for advertising
- Usage analytics or behavioral tracking data
- Photos, contacts, or other device data

## Third-Party Services

### Google Play Games Services
We use Google Play Games Services for leaderboards. When you opt in to leaderboards, your score and Google Play Games display name may be visible to other players. Google's Privacy Policy applies to their services: https://policies.google.com/privacy

### Google Play Billing
In-app purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. We do not have access to your payment information. Google's Privacy Policy applies: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Data Retention

- Game progress is stored locally on your device until you uninstall the Game or clear app data
- Leaderboard scores are retained by Google Play Games according to their policies
- We do not maintain servers that store your personal data

## Children's Privacy

Edinburgh Rough Streets is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The Game contains themes (simulated drug dealing) that are intended for mature audiences.

## Data Security

Your game data is stored locally on your device using Android's Room database with standard encryption. We do not transmit personal data over the internet.

## Your Rights

You can:
- Delete all local game data by uninstalling the Game or clearing app data
- Opt out of Google Play Games leaderboards at any time
- Manage your Google Play Games data through your Google account settings

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
